Accent

An accent is that little personal touch which makes a dress designed by a couturier your own dress. It is of great importance.

The accent must always have the touch of your personality ...

The place you put a clip ...

The way you tie a bow ...

How you fold your scarf ...

The color you choose for your flower ...


With your personal feeling no one can do it better than you ...


But, be careful, one accent is enough. And if you choose a color accent, select it with great care—and remember that except in the hands of an expert two colors in one outfit is enough.
