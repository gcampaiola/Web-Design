Originally from Rome, Italy (b.1964), Luca Campaiola is a passionate amateur photographer that predominantly captures the architecture and natural wonders he encounters on his international travels. An architect by profession, Luca has been working and residing in Southampton, New York with his wife and two daughters for 22 years. He cherishes photography as both a personal creative outlet and a means to connect with his daughter Giulia.
